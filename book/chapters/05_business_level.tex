Edeka operates as a cooperative (Genossenschaft) with a unique business model:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Structure}: Cooperative with independent retailers
    \item \textbf{Vertical integration}: Own production and logistics
    \item \textbf{Private labels}: Strong brands like "Gut \& Günstig", "Edeka Bio"
    \item \textbf{Diversification}: Food, non-food, services
\end{itemize}

艾德卡作为合作社（Genossenschaft）运营，具有独特的商业模式：
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{结构}：由独立零售商组成的合作社
    \item \textbf{垂直整合}：自有生产和物流
    \item \textbf{自有品牌}：强大的品牌如"Gut \& Günstig"、"Edeka Bio"
    \item \textbf{多元化}：食品、非食品、服务
\end{itemize}
